# Vendor Agreement — Acme Analytics LLC and Birchwood Retail Inc.

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

## 1. Services

1.1 Acme Analytics LLC ("Vendor") shall provide the retail analytics platform and related support services to Birchwood Retail Inc. ("Customer") as described in one or more Order Forms.

## 2. Fees

2.1 Customer shall pay the fees in each Order Form annually in advance. Invoices are due within thirty (30) days of receipt.

2.2 Vendor may change its fees and these terms at any time by posting updated pricing or terms on its website. Customer's continued use of the Services after posting constitutes acceptance of the change.

2.3 Overdue amounts accrue interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month.

## 3. Data and Security

3.1 Vendor may use Customer Data, including point-of-sale and customer loyalty data, to improve its products, create aggregated benchmarks, and train machine-learning models offered to other customers.

3.2 Vendor shall notify Customer within twenty-four (24) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to Customer Data, describing the data affected and the remediation steps taken.

## 4. Term and Termination

4.1 This Agreement has an initial term of three (3) years and renews automatically for successive three (3) year terms unless Customer gives notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term.

4.2 Vendor may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time on five (5) days' written notice. Customer may terminate only for Vendor's uncured material breach.

4.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice.

## 5. Liability and Indemnity

5.1 VENDOR'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE THREE (3) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM. CUSTOMER'S LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IS NOT LIMITED.

5.2 Customer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Vendor against any and all claims, losses, damages and expenses arising out of or relating to Customer's use of the Services.

5.3 Vendor shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the platform infringes a United States patent, copyright or trademark, and shall pay any damages finally awarded.

## 6. Governing Law

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.
