About Recital

Recital is a retrieval-augmented question-answering system for contracts, built by Badee Khalbouss. It answers from the agreements only, cites the clause behind every claim, and declines when the agreements don't cover a question.

How an answer is made

  1. Split by clause. Each agreement is cut at its numbered clauses. Every clause keeps its agreement and section name.
  2. Retrieve. The question is embedded with bge-small and compared with all clauses. Keyword (BM25) and hybrid search are available to compare.
  3. Answer. A local qwen2.5:7b, served by Ollama, writes an answer from the retrieved clauses only and tags each sentence with its source. Tags that don't match a retrieved clause are discarded.
  4. Measure. Every change is scored on hand-labelled questions and logged to MLflow.
