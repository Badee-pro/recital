# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement — Harbor Freight Analytics, Inc. and Lumen Biosystems LLC

Effective Date: March 3, 2026

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any non-public business, technical, or financial information disclosed by one party (the "Discloser") to the other party (the "Recipient"), whether orally, visually, or in writing, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.2 "Purpose" means evaluating a potential collaboration in which Harbor Freight Analytics would provide demand-forecasting models for Lumen Biosystems' reagent supply chain.

1.3 "Representatives" means a party's employees, officers, directors, attorneys, accountants, and financial advisors who need to know Confidential Information for the Purpose.

## 2. Obligations of the Recipient

2.1 The Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and shall not use it to compete with the Discloser or to develop any product that is substantially similar to a product of the Discloser.

2.2 The Recipient shall protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

2.3 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its Representatives, provided that each Representative is bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement. The Recipient remains liable for any breach by its Representatives.

## 3. Exclusions

3.1 Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient; (b) was rightfully known to the Recipient without restriction before receipt from the Discloser; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information, as shown by contemporaneous written records.

## 4. Compelled Disclosure

4.1 If the Recipient is required by law, subpoena, or court order to disclose Confidential Information, the Recipient shall give the Discloser prompt written notice, to the extent legally permitted, at least ten (10) business days before disclosure, so that the Discloser may seek a protective order. The Recipient shall disclose only the portion of Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required.

## 5. Term and Duration of Obligations

5.1 This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years from the Effective Date unless terminated earlier by either party on thirty (30) days' written notice.

5.2 The Recipient's confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement, except that obligations relating to trade secrets survive for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 6. Return and Destruction

6.1 Within fifteen (15) days after the Discloser's written request, or upon termination, the Recipient shall return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing signed by an officer. The Recipient may retain one archival copy solely to demonstrate compliance and copies held in automatic electronic backups, which remain subject to this Agreement.

## 7. No License; No Warranty

7.1 Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any license or right in the Discloser's Confidential Information, patents, or other intellectual property. All Confidential Information is provided "AS IS" without warranty of any kind.

## 8. Non-Solicitation

8.1 For twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, neither party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other party with whom it had contact in connection with the Purpose. General solicitations not targeted at such employees, such as public job postings, do not violate this section.

## 9. Remedies

9.1 Each party acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

## 10. Governing Law

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.
