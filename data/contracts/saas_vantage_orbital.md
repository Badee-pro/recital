# SaaS Subscription Agreement — Vantage Retail Group and Orbital Insights, Inc.

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## 1. Subscription

1.1 Orbital Insights ("Vendor") grants Vantage Retail Group ("Subscriber") a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Subscription Term to access and use the Orbital pricing-intelligence platform (the "Platform") for Subscriber's internal business purposes, for up to 250 named users.

1.2 Subscriber shall not (a) reverse engineer the Platform; (b) resell or sublicense access; (c) use the Platform to build a competing product; or (d) exceed the usage limits stated in the Order Form. Additional users may be added at the then-current per-user price, prorated for the remainder of the term.

## 2. Fees

2.1 Subscriber shall pay an annual subscription fee of $480,000, invoiced annually in advance and due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

2.2 Upon renewal, Vendor may increase fees by no more than the lesser of seven percent (7%) or the percentage increase in the U.S. Consumer Price Index over the preceding twelve months.

2.3 If any amount is more than thirty (30) days overdue, Vendor may suspend access to the Platform after giving Subscriber ten (10) days' written notice.

## 3. Service Levels

3.1 Vendor shall make the Platform available 99.9% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance and limited to eight (8) hours per month.

3.2 If availability falls below 99.9%, Subscriber is entitled to a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee. If availability falls below 99.0%, the credit is 25% of the monthly fee. Credits must be requested within thirty (30) days after the end of the affected month.

3.3 If availability falls below 99.0% in any three (3) months within a rolling twelve-month period, Subscriber may terminate this Agreement and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term. Service credits and this termination right are Subscriber's sole remedies for availability failures.

## 4. Data

4.1 Subscriber retains all rights in data it uploads to the Platform ("Subscriber Data"). Vendor may use Subscriber Data only to provide the Platform and may not use it to train machine-learning models offered to other customers.

4.2 Vendor may collect aggregated, de-identified usage statistics that do not identify Subscriber or any individual, and may use them to improve the Platform.

4.3 Upon termination, Vendor shall make Subscriber Data available for export for sixty (60) days, after which it shall delete Subscriber Data.

## 5. Term and Renewal

5.1 The initial Subscription Term is two (2) years from the Effective Date. Thereafter, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the current term.

5.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written notice.

## 6. Limitation of Liability

6.1 EXCEPT FOR SUBSCRIBER'S PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS AND EITHER PARTY'S BREACH OF SECTION 4, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID BY SUBSCRIBER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

6.2 For breach of Section 4 (Data), Vendor's aggregate liability shall not exceed two million dollars ($2,000,000).

## 7. Assignment

7.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except that either party may assign it without consent to a successor in a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, provided the successor is not a direct competitor of the non-assigning party.

## 8. Governing Law

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. The state and federal courts in San Francisco County, California, have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute.
