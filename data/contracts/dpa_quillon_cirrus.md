# Data Processing Addendum — Quillon Software, Inc. and Cirrus Health Partners

Effective Date: May 1, 2026

## 1. Roles and Scope

1.1 Cirrus Health Partners ("Controller") engages Quillon Software ("Processor") to process Personal Data on Controller's behalf in connection with the patient-scheduling platform described in the underlying services agreement. Processor shall process Personal Data only on Controller's documented instructions.

1.2 Categories of data subjects include Controller's patients, prospective patients, and staff. Categories of Personal Data include names, contact details, appointment history, and insurance identifiers. Processor shall not process special categories of data, such as diagnoses, unless the Controller expressly instructs it in writing.

## 2. Security Measures

2.1 Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures, including encryption of Personal Data at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, and logging of access to Personal Data retained for at least one (1) year.

2.2 Processor shall ensure that all personnel with access to Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations and receive data-protection training at least annually.

## 3. Sub-processors

3.1 Controller grants general authorization for Processor to engage sub-processors. Processor shall maintain a current list of sub-processors and notify Controller at least thirty (30) days before adding or replacing a sub-processor.

3.2 Controller may object to a new sub-processor on reasonable data-protection grounds within fifteen (15) days of notice. If the parties cannot resolve the objection, Controller may terminate the affected services without penalty.

3.3 Processor shall impose data-protection obligations on each sub-processor that are no less protective than those in this Addendum and remains fully liable for each sub-processor's performance.

## 4. Personal Data Breach

4.1 Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach. The notice shall describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address it.

4.2 Processor shall cooperate with Controller's investigation and shall not notify regulators or data subjects about a breach affecting Controller's data without Controller's prior written approval, unless required by law.

## 5. Audits

5.1 Processor shall make available its most recent SOC 2 Type II report upon request. Controller may, no more than once per year and on at least thirty (30) days' notice, conduct an on-site audit at its own expense, unless the audit follows a Personal Data Breach, in which case the frequency limit does not apply.

## 6. International Transfers

6.1 Processor shall not transfer Personal Data outside the United States without Controller's prior written consent. Where consent is given, the parties shall put in place a transfer mechanism recognized under applicable law.

## 7. Data Subject Requests

7.1 Processor shall forward to Controller any request from a data subject to exercise privacy rights within five (5) business days of receipt and shall not respond to the request directly unless authorized by Controller. Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to enable Controller to respond.

## 8. Deletion and Return

8.1 Within thirty (30) days after the end of the services, Processor shall, at Controller's choice, return all Personal Data in a commonly used machine-readable format or delete it, and shall delete existing copies from backups within ninety (90) days, unless retention is required by law.

## 9. Liability

9.1 Each party's liability under this Addendum is subject to the limitation of liability in the underlying services agreement, except that Processor's liability for a Personal Data Breach caused by its failure to comply with Section 2 is capped at three times (3x) the fees paid in the twelve (12) months before the breach.
