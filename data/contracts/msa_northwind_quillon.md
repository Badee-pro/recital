# Master Services Agreement — Northwind Logistics Corp. and Quillon Software, Inc.

Effective Date: January 15, 2026

## 1. Services

1.1 Quillon Software ("Provider") shall provide the software development, integration, and support services described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOWs") executed by Northwind Logistics ("Customer"). Each SOW is governed by this Agreement. If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls unless the SOW expressly states that it overrides a specific section.

1.2 Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards, using personnel with appropriate skill and training.

## 2. Fees and Payment

2.1 Customer shall pay the fees set out in each SOW. Provider shall invoice monthly in arrears. Invoices are due within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

2.2 Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

2.3 Customer may dispute an invoice in good faith by notifying Provider in writing within fifteen (15) days of receipt, describing the disputed amount and the reason. The parties shall work to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days. Customer shall pay any undisputed portion on time.

2.4 Provider may increase its hourly rates no more than once per calendar year, by no more than five percent (5%), on at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice.

## 3. Intellectual Property

3.1 Upon full payment, Customer owns all deliverables specifically created for Customer under an SOW ("Deliverables"), including all intellectual property rights in them.

3.2 Provider retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, libraries, and know-how ("Provider Materials"). To the extent Provider Materials are incorporated into a Deliverable, Provider grants Customer a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use them as part of that Deliverable.

## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information secret and use it only to perform or receive the Services. These obligations continue for five (5) years after termination of this Agreement.

## 5. Warranties

5.1 Provider warrants that for ninety (90) days after acceptance, each Deliverable will materially conform to its specifications in the applicable SOW. Customer's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is for Provider to re-perform the non-conforming Services at no charge or, if Provider cannot do so within thirty (30) days, a refund of the fees paid for the non-conforming Deliverable.

5.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 6. Indemnification

6.1 Provider shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that a Deliverable infringes a United States patent, copyright, or trademark, and shall pay any resulting damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement. This obligation does not apply to infringement caused by Customer's modifications or by combination with items not supplied by Provider.

6.2 Customer shall defend Provider against any third-party claim arising from data or materials Customer supplies to Provider.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

7.1 NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THEIR POSSIBILITY.

7.2 EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

7.3 The limits in Sections 7.1 and 7.2 do not apply to a party's indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality, or gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 This Agreement has an initial term of three (3) years from the Effective Date and renews automatically for successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

8.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.

8.3 Customer may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice, provided Customer pays for Services performed through the termination date.

## 9. Insurance

9.1 Provider shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and professional liability (errors and omissions) insurance of at least $5,000,000 in the aggregate throughout the term.

## 10. Governing Law and Disputes

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. Before filing suit, the parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation between executives for at least twenty (20) days. Any unresolved dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association in New York, New York, before a single arbitrator.
